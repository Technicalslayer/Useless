# Useless! A Unity 2D project made for GMTK 2023 game jam and then polished into a small finished game.
In Useless!, you play as the lowly spy of an evil overlord. 
Your duties are to motivate the villain’s minions to work, spy on the hero of the land, and report your findings to your lord via messenger crow as flawlessly as possible, or risk facing your greatest fear: unemployment!
